POST
/api/v1/meetings
Create Meeting


Schedule a new meeting on the current user's personal calendar.

link_or_location is dual-purpose: a join URL for online platforms (zoom/teams/google_meet) or a venue/address when platform is in_person. The frontend picks which to ask for based on platform.
